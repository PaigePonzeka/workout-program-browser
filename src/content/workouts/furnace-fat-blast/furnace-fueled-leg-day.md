A lower body heatwave focusing on glutes, quads, and hamstrings with burning squats and tempo work. Reinforces the flame theme and fires up foundational strength.
## Warm-Up

7 min cardio + mobility:
- 2 min incline walk or bike
- 10 walking lunges (total)
- 10 glute bridges
- 10 bodyweight good mornings

## Strength or Skill

Barbell Back Squat
- 4x8 @ RPE 7
- Rest 2-3 min

Dumbbell Romanian Deadlift
- 3x12 controlled reps

Alternating Reverse Lunge (DBs optional)
- 2x12/leg

## Metcon

Chipper (20 min cap):
- 30 goblet squats (40/25 lbs)
- 400m run or 30 cal row/bike
- 40 kettlebell swings (53/35 lbs)
- 400m run or 30 cal row/bike
- 50 walking lunges (bodyweight)

Scaling: Reduce KB weight, shorten runs/row/bike, use dumbbell swings

## Cool-Down

- Couch stretch: 60 sec/side
- Standing forward fold: 75 sec
- Figure-4 glute stretch: 45 sec/side

## Core / Optional Finisher

3 x 30 sec side plank (each side)

## Coaching Notes

Keep squat depth consistent and quality high. Move steadily through chipper; avoid all-out sprinting.

### Week-over-Week Progression
- Week 1: 4x8 squats @ RPE 7, base chipper
- Week 2: 4x10 squats @ RPE 7, +5 reps per chipper section
- Week 3: 5x6 squats @ RPE 8, keep chipper, try faster splits
- Week 4: 3x8 squats @ RPE 6, reduce chipper by 10 reps/movement

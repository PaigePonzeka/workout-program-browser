A fiery pull session focused on back and biceps, using a roasting mix of horizontal and vertical pulls. Keeps the Furnace Fat Blast theme hot while maintaining pulling variety for balance.
## Warm-Up

6 min (for quality):
- 6 cal ski-erg/rower
- 10 banded straight-arm pull-downs
- 8 band face pulls
- 10 scapular lat pull-downs

## Strength or Skill

Barbell Bent-Over Row
- 4x8 @ RPE 7
- Rest 2 min

Lat Pull-Down (wide grip)
- 4x10 @ moderate weight

Alternating Dumbbell Hammer Curl
- 3x12 each arm

## Metcon

10 min AMRAP:
- 12 alternating renegade rows (with dumbbells)
- 10 kneeling banded lat pull-downs
- 20 double-unders or 40 singles (or 30 sec fast jump rope)

Scaling: Use lighter DBs, substitute band rows for renegade rows, single-unders for double-unders

## Cool-Down

- Doorway lat stretch: 60 sec/side
- Banded pec/shoulder stretch: 45 sec/side
- Neck lateral stretch: 45 sec/side

## Core / Optional Finisher

Accumulate 1:30 plank (broken as needed)

## Coaching Notes

Prioritize control on rows and pulling movements; chase steady pacing in the AMRAP.

### Week-over-Week Progression
- Week 1: 4x8 rows, 4x10 lat pull-down, 10 min AMRAP
- Week 2: 4x10 rows, 4x12 lat pull-down, 12 min AMRAP
- Week 3: 4x8 (add 5-10 lbs to rows), 5x8 lat pull-down, 10 min AMRAP (faster pace)
- Week 4: 3x10 (deload), 3x10 lat pull-down, 8 min AMRAP

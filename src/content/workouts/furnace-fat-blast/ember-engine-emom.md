A conditioning-focused inferno day that sparks fat loss with full-body interval training. Stokes the program’s fire theme and primes the metabolic engine.
## Warm-Up

5 min easy cardio (row, bike, or jog)
Then 2 rounds:
- 12 jumping jacks
- 10 air squats
- 8 walkouts

## Metcon

18 min EMOM:
- Minute 1: 15/12 calorie row or bike (or 200m run)
- Minute 2: 12 dumbbell thrusters (25/15 lbs)
- Minute 3: 15 kettlebell swings (53/35 lbs)

Scaling: Bike/row/run—reduce calories to 12/8, thrusters with less weight or squat to box, lighter kettlebell swings

## Cool-Down

- Pigeon stretch: 45 sec/side
- Hamstring stretch: 60 sec/side
- Supine twist: 45 sec/side

## Core / Optional Finisher

Tabata (4 min): Alternating plank and hollow hold (20 sec on/10 sec off)

## Coaching Notes

Work hard in each interval; choose weights to finish in 40-45 sec. 

### Week-over-Week Progression
- Week 1: 18 min EMOM, moderate reps
- Week 2: EMOM add 1-2 reps per movement if unbroken, or increase load slightly
- Week 3: 20 min EMOM, same or similar loads
- Week 4: 15 min EMOM (deload), choose lighter/easier versions

A push-day inferno stoking up chest, shoulders, and triceps with fiery pressing and relentless intensity. Supports the Furnace Fat Blast theme by igniting upper body power early in the week.
## Warm-Up

6 min (for quality):
- 8 calorie bike/row
- 12 band pull-aparts
- 8 push-ups (knee if needed)
- 10 shoulder taps (total)

## Strength or Skill

Barbell Bench Press
- 4 sets x 8 reps @ RPE 7
- Rest 2 min

Standing Dumbbell Arnold Press
- 3 sets x 10 reps @ moderate load
- Rest 90 sec

## Metcon

12 min AMRAP:
- 12 dumbbell push press (35/20 lbs)
- 10 box jumps (20/16")
- 8 hand-release push-ups (scale: regular push-ups or hands-elevated)

Scaling: Reduce dumbbell weight, step-ups for box jumps, knee or incline push-ups

## Cool-Down

- Doorway chest stretch: 60 sec/side
- Triceps overhead stretch: 45 sec/side
- Seated shoulder stretch: 60 sec

## Core / Optional Finisher

2 rounds:
- 20 cross-body mountain climbers
- 10 slow tempo dead bugs

## Coaching Notes

Keep all bench sets smooth; leave 2 reps in the tank. Metcon: Keep good form on presses, jump safely. 

### Week-over-Week Progression
- Week 1: 4x8 bench @ RPE 7, 12 min AMRAP
- Week 2: 4x8 bench @ RPE 7.5 (add 5-10 lbs), 14 min AMRAP
- Week 3: 5x6 bench @ RPE 8, 12 min AMRAP (increase DB weight)
- Week 4: 3x8 bench @ RPE 6-6.5 (deload), 10 min AMRAP

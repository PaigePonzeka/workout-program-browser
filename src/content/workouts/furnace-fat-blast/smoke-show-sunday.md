A rotating full-body burn blending light weights, cardio, and core for the ultimate calorie inferno. Keeps the furnace theme flaming with spicy movement combos.
## Warm-Up

5 min mixed cardio (row, jog, or jump rope)
Then 2 rounds:
- 6 inchworms
- 12 alternating spider lunges
- 8 air squats

## Strength or Skill

Dumbbell Walking Lunge
- 3x16 steps (light-moderate)

Standing Band Pull-Apart
- 3x15 reps

Alternating Side Plank Reach-Through
- 3x10/side

## Metcon

15 min AMRAP:
- 10 dumbbell snatches (alternating, 30/20 lbs)
- 12 push-ups (knee/incline as needed)
- 15 wall balls (20/14 lbs, scale: med ball thrusters)
- 100m run or 10 cal bike/row

Scaling: Reduce DB/wall ball weight, sub incline push-ups or bodyweight squats

## Cool-Down

- Child's pose: 75 sec
- Seated hamstring stretch: 60 sec/leg
- Cross-body shoulder stretch: 45 sec/side

## Core / Optional Finisher

Tabata: Med ball Russian twists (20/14 lbs, or bodyweight as needed)

## Coaching Notes

Push the pace but keep reps crisp on all movements. Use lighter weights if technique breaks.

### Week-over-Week Progression
- Week 1: All reps as listed above, 15 min AMRAP
- Week 2: Add 1-2 reps/movement on AMRAP OR slightly heavier DB
- Week 3: 17 min AMRAP with normal reps
- Week 4: 12 min AMRAP, lighter/weaker movements as needed (deload)

Take flight on pull day, focusing on upper-back and grip strength with creative rows and pulldowns — your ticket to a mighty wingspan!
## Warm-Up

5 min rower or ski erg

2 rounds for quality:
- 12 banded straight-arm pull-downs
- 10 scap retractions (lat pull-down bar)
- 8 dowel dislocates
- 10 jumping jacks

## Strength or Skill

Barbell Bent-Over Row
- 4 sets x 8 reps @ RPE 7
- Rest 2 min

Lat Pull-down (neutral grip)
- 3 sets x 10 reps
- Rest 90 sec

Single-Arm Dumbbell Row
- 3 sets x 10 reps per arm
- Rest 60 sec

## Metcon

EMOM x 16 min:
- Minute 1: 8 banded lat pull-downs (heavy)
- Minute 2: 10 dumbbell hammer curls
- Minute 3: 8 kettlebell gorilla rows (4/side, moderate weight)
- Minute 4: 20 sec bike/row sprint

Repeat for 4 cycles.

Scaling: Use lighter bands or reduced reps for pull-downs; use lighter DBs/KBs; bike/row at steady pace if sprint is too intense.

## Cool-Down

- Cross-body shoulder stretch: 1 min/side
- Cat-cow stretch: 1 min
- Wrist flexor stretch: 45 sec/side

## Core / Optional Finisher

2 rounds:
- 20 Russian twists
- 15 bird-dogs

## Coaching Notes

Keep your torso braced during rows. Use a thumbless (false) grip for accessory lat activation if comfortable. Keep quality on every EMOM rep.

### Week-over-Week Progression
- Week 1: 4x8 rows @ RPE 7, 16 min EMOM
- Week 2: 4x8 rows @ RPE 7.5 (increase weight 2.5-5 lbs), add 2 reps to hammer curls/gorilla rows
- Week 3: 5x6 rows @ RPE 8, increase band/DB/KB resistance
- Week 4: 3x10 rows @ RPE 6, reduce volume on EMOM for freshness

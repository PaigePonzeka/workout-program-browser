Bounce into the weekend! This amphibious full-body session reinforces all major movement patterns without overloading any one area—perfect jungle recovery fun.
## Warm-Up

5 min jump rope or bike

2 rounds:
- 10 banded good mornings
- 8 tall kneeling overhead reaches
- 8 frog hops (squat jump, land soft)

## Strength or Skill

Dumbbell Clean and Press (single arm, alternating)
- 4 sets x 8 reps/arm @ moderate weight
- Rest 90 sec

Superset:
- Bulgarian Split Squat: 10 reps/leg
- Bent-over Reverse Fly: 12 reps
3 rounds, rest as needed

## Metcon

EMOM x 15 min:
- Minute 1: 14 box step-overs (alt legs, light DBs in hands optional)
- Minute 2: 10 push-ups (elevate hands/bench for scaling)
- Minute 3: 15 kettlebell swings

Repeat 5 rounds.

Scaling:
- Lower KB/DB weight, decrease reps, step to box, use knees or incline for push-ups as needed.

## Cool-Down

- Frog stretch: 1 min
- Child's pose: 1 min
- Standing quad stretch: 1 min/leg

## Core / Optional Finisher

2 rounds:
- 30 sec dead bug hold
- 20 standing twists

## Coaching Notes

Focus on smooth movement, not maximal loads—Friday is about quality, not burnout. Modify box height and weights to move with precision.

### Week-over-Week Progression
- Week 1: As above
- Week 2: Add 2 reps/EMOM exercise
- Week 3: Add a 6th round to EMOM, increase DB weight on cleans by 5 lbs if able
- Week 4: Deload: decrease all reps by 25% (except cleans at Week 1 weight)

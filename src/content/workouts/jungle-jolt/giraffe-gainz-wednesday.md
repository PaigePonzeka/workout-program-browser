Reach new heights on leg day! Focus on strong, tall squats and hingy movements so your lower half can bound through the jungle.
## Warm-Up

5 min incline treadmill walk OR stairmaster

2 rounds:
- 12 walking lunges
- 10 bodyweight squats
- 10 banded hamstring curls
- 8 lateral leg swings

## Strength or Skill

Barbell Back Squat
- 4 sets x 8 reps @ RPE 7
- Rest 2.5 min

Romanian Deadlift (Barbell or Dumbbell)
- 3 sets x 10 reps @ moderate weight
- Rest 90 sec

## Metcon

Chipper (Time Cap: 18 min):
- 40 goblet squats (moderate DB/KB)
- 30 box step-ups (20/16")
- 20 single-arm kettlebell swings (10/arm)
- 40 walking lunges (bodyweight)

Scaling:
- Use lighter goblet loads or reduce box height/step reps; sub bodyweight glute bridges for KB swings if needed.

## Cool-Down

- Seated hamstring stretch: 1 min/side
- Couch stretch: 45 sec/side
- Frog stretch: 1 min

## Core / Optional Finisher

2 rounds:
- 20 standing banded anti-rotation holds (10/side)
- 20 sec suitcase carry (per side)

## Coaching Notes

Keep depth and tension on back squats—chest high like a giraffe! For chipper, break sets early to avoid form breakdown.

### Week-over-Week Progression
- Week 1: 4x8 squats @ RPE 7, 18 min chipper
- Week 2: 4x8 @ RPE 7.5 (+5-10 lbs), reduce chipper cap by 1 min
- Week 3: 5x6 @ RPE 8, increase goblet/KB weight on chipper
- Week 4: 3x10 squats @ RPE 6, short chipper (halve reps)

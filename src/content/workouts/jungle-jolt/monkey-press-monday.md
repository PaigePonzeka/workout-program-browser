Swing into the week with an energetic push session, blending barbell and dumbbell presses with jungle-speed intervals to stoke strength and torch calories.
## Warm-Up

5 min brisk bike or treadmill walk

2 rounds for quality:
- 10 band pull-aparts
- 10 shoulder circles (each way)
- 8 push-ups (knees if needed)
- 10 glute bridges

## Strength or Skill

Barbell Overhead Press
- 4 sets x 8 reps @ RPE 7
- Rest 2 min

Incline Dumbbell Bench Press
- 3 sets x 10 reps @ moderate weight
- Rest 90 sec

## Metcon

12 min AMRAP:
- 10 dumbbell push press (per arm, 35/20 lbs, scale as needed)
- 12 box jumps (24/20")
- 15 mountain climbers (per side)

Scaling:
- Use lighter DBs or reduce reps for push press; box step-ups for jumps; elevated mountain climbers as needed.

## Cool-Down

- Chest/doorway stretch: 45 sec/side
- Overhead triceps stretch: 45 sec/side
- Thread-the-needle stretch: 1 min/side

## Core / Optional Finisher

3 rounds:
- 15 hollow rocks
- 20 sec plank

## Coaching Notes

Focus on keeping a strong vertical line in presses. For the AMRAP, choose a dumbbell weight you can move explosively. Step up or down box height as needed.

### Week-over-Week Progression
- Week 1: 4x8 OH press @ RPE 7, 12 min AMRAP
- Week 2: 4x8 OH press @ RPE 7.5 (+2.5-5 lbs), 14 min AMRAP
- Week 3: 5x6 OH press @ RPE 8, increase DB/box height if able
- Week 4: 3x10 OH press @ RPE 6, 10 min AMRAP (deload)

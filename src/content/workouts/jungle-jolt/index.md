  ## Program Overview 
  Welcome to Jungle Jolt—a tropical-themed 4-week CrossFit program designed to supercharge fat loss and conditioning while helping you swing through strength plateaus! Each workout channels jungle inspiration with fun flora-and-fauna puns, combining compound lifts, creative circuits, and animal-energy conditioning. Every week adds intensity or volume, helping you shed fat, build muscle, and unleash your wild side.

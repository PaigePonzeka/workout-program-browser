Engine day! Get agile like a tiger—this conditioning trek blends cardio with animal-inspired moves for a high-energy, sweat-soaked session to boost your fat-burning engine.
## Warm-Up

5 min any monostructural (row, bike, treadmill)

Then 2 rounds for quality:
- 10 air squats
- 10 jumping jacks
- 10 alternating Spiderman lunges
- 30 sec high knees

## Metcon

20 min AMRAP:
- 300 m row/bike/ski (any)
- 10 dumbbell thrusters (25/15 lbs, pairs)
- 12 burpees
- 20 walking lunges (bodyweight)

Scaling:
- Lower thruster load, reduce burpees to 8, sub step-back lunges if needed; advanced: increase burpees to 15 or lunges to 30 reps per round.

## Cool-Down

- Forward fold stretch: 1 min
- Quad stretch: 45 sec/side
- Downward dog: 1 min

## Core / Optional Finisher

Tabata: 20 sec on/10 sec off x 4 each
- Flutter kicks
- Seated knees-to-chest crunches

## Coaching Notes

Keep steady pace—this is a longer AMRAP, so focus on smooth transitions. Choose a thruster weight you can do unbroken for at least first 2 rounds.

### Week-over-Week Progression
- Week 1: 20 min AMRAP as written
- Week 2: Increase lunges to 24 reps/round
- Week 3: Advanced: add DB snatch (8/side) each round; otherwise increase pace
- Week 4: 16 min AMRAP (with reduced reps for maintenance)

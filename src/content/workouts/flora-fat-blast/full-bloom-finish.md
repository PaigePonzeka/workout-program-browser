Rotating full-body circuit, mixing compound lifts and engine finish to maximize fat burn and wrap up the week's natural growth theme.
## Warm-Up
5 min choice cardio (row, bike, jog)
2 rounds:
- 10 banded good mornings
- 10 reverse lunges
- 8 knee push-ups

## Strength or Skill
Dumbbell Clean & Press
- 3 sets x 8 reps @ RPE 7 (per arm)
- Rest 90 sec

Goblet Squat
- 3 sets x 10 reps @ RPE 7

Lat Pull-Down (supinated grip)
- 3 sets x 12 reps

## Metcon
12 min AMRAP:
- 10 alternating DB snatches (35/20 lbs)
- 12 calorie bike/row
- 12 sit-ups

Scaling: Lower DB weight; sub KB swing for snatch; reduce calories to 8. Advanced: 45/25 lb DB or add 8 push-ups each round.

## Cool-Down
- Kneeling hip flexor stretch: 45 sec/side
- Child's pose: 60 sec
- Cross-body shoulder stretch: 30 sec/side

## Core / Optional Finisher
Tabata Russian twists (8 rounds 20:10)

## Coaching Notes
Cycle between good quality cleans and presses, smooth transitions in full-body metcon.

### Week-over-Week Progression
- Week 1: Prescribed sets/reps; 12 min AMRAP
- Week 2: Add 1 set to each lift; 14 min AMRAP
- Week 3: Increase DB weight or pace; keep 12 min AMRAP
- Week 4: Reduce load/intensity, focus on clean reps, cap AMRAP at 10 min

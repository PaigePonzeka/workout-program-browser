A vibrant push day designed to grow pressing power and upper body endurance, setting strong roots for the program's blossoming theme.
## Warm-Up
5 min easy bike or row
Then 2 rounds (for quality):
- 10 band pull-aparts
- 10 push-up to down-dog
- 8 arm circles each direction

## Strength or Skill
Barbell Bench Press
- 4 sets x 8 reps @ RPE 7
- Rest 2 min

Dumbbell Seated Shoulder Press
- 3 sets x 10 reps @ RPE 7
- Rest 90 sec

Lat Pull-Down (wide grip)
- 3 sets x 12 reps @ RPE 7

## Metcon
13 min AMRAP:
- 8 dumbbell thrusters (35/20 lbs)
- 12 box step-overs (24/20")
- 16 banded tricep pushdowns

Scaling: Lower DB weight, substitute step-ups for step-overs, use a lighter band for pushdowns. For advanced: use 40/25 lb DBs and box jumps.

## Cool-Down
- Pec stretch in doorway: 45 sec/side
- Seated shoulder stretch: 60 sec
- Thread the needle stretch: 30 sec/side

## Core / Optional Finisher
2 rounds for quality:
- 12 plank shoulder taps
- 30 sec hollow rock

## Coaching Notes
Emphasize strict form in all presses and smooth transitions in the metcon. Focus on full lockout and maintaining shoulder engagement.

### Week-over-Week Progression
- Week 1: 4x8 bench @ RPE 7; 13 min AMRAP
- Week 2: 4x8 bench @ RPE 7.5 (add 5-10 lbs); 15 min AMRAP
- Week 3: 5x6 bench @ RPE 8; 13 min AMRAP (increase DB weight)
- Week 4: 3x10 bench @ RPE 6 (lighter than week 1, quality focus); 10 min AMRAP

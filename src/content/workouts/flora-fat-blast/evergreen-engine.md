Conditioning day inspired by the unyielding energy of ancient forests: focus on full-body aerobic stamina and efficient pacing.
## Warm-Up
5 min fast-paced walk/jog
2 rounds:
- 20 jumping jacks
- 10 air squats
- 10 arm swings

## Metcon
Chipper for time (cap: 22 min):
- 800m run or 1k row
- 40 wall balls (20/14 lbs)
- 30 step-back lunges (bodyweight)
- 20 push-ups (can incline)
- 60 calories bike/row
- 40 box step-ups
- 20 burpees

Scaling: Decrease wall ball or step-up height, use lighter wall ball, incline/bench push-ups. Advanced: Add 20 lb vest OR increase bike/row to 70 cals.

## Cool-Down
- Forward fold: 60 sec
- Standing quad stretch: 45 sec/side
- Reclining spinal twist: 60 sec

## Core / Optional Finisher
60 sec plank hold (hollow position), optional: 30 sec side plank/side

## Coaching Notes
Move steadily, break reps early. Prioritize full ROM in all calisthenics. Hydrate after longer duration work.

### Week-over-Week Progression
- Week 1: Standard rep chipper; 22 min cap
- Week 2: Add 10 reps to wall balls/step-ups; 24 min cap
- Week 3: Keep reps, try to improve time from prior week
- Week 4: Decrease to 70% rep volume, focus on max quality and pace

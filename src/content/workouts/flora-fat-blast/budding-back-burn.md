Pull day dedicated to building strong lats and grip—these pulling motions echo the energizing pull of spring roots underground.
## Warm-Up
5 min rower or ski erg (steady pace)
Then 2 rounds:
- 10 band rows (with pause)
- 10 scap retractions (on pull-down station)
- 8 band face-pulls

## Strength or Skill
Barbell Pendlay Row
- 4 sets x 8 reps @ RPE 7
- Rest 2 min

Lat Pull-Down (neutral grip)
- 3 sets x 12 reps @ RPE 7
- Rest 90 sec

Dumbbell Bicep Curl
- 3 sets x 12 reps (controlled)

## Metcon
15 min EMOM:
- Minute 1: 10 single-arm kettlebell rows (5/side)
- Minute 2: 12 banded straight-arm pulldowns
- Minute 3: 18 mountain climbers

Scaling: Decrease KB weight, reduce reps. Advanced: add odd-object farmer carry (35/25 lbs DB, 25m walk) to Minute 3.

## Cool-Down
- Child's pose: 60 sec
- Cross-body lat stretch: 45 sec/side
- Seated wrist flexor stretch: 30 sec/side

## Core / Optional Finisher
Tabata (8x20:10) Hollow Hold

## Coaching Notes
Keep shoulder blades actively engaged on all rows. Focus on posture and control—don’t rush EMOM reps.

### Week-over-Week Progression
- Week 1: Rows 4x8 @ RPE 7; 15 min EMOM
- Week 2: Rows 4x10 @ RPE 7 (same or +5 lbs); 16 min EMOM (add 1 round)
- Week 3: Rows 5x6 @ RPE 8; 18 min EMOM (increase KB weight if form allows)
- Week 4: Rows 3x12 @ RPE 6 (lighter, slow, perfect); 12 min EMOM

Leg day grounded in compound lifts and dynamic lower body circuits, strengthening your stems for the rest of the week.
## Warm-Up
6 min easy walk or stairmaster
Then 2 rounds:
- 10 glute bridges
- 8 lunges/side
- 10 banded lateral walks

## Strength or Skill
Barbell Back Squat
- 4 sets x 8 reps @ RPE 7
- Rest 2-3 min

Romanian Deadlift (DB or BB)
- 3 sets x 10 reps @ RPE 7

Bulgarian Split Squat (bodyweight)
- 2 sets x 12 reps/side

## Metcon
16 min AMRAP:
- 12 goblet squats (40/25 lbs)
- 10 box jumps or step-ups (24/20")
- 10 kettlebell swings (53/35 lbs)

Scaling: Reduce weights; substitute step-ups for jumps; KB swings at eye level if needed. Advanced: Increase KB/squat load.

## Cool-Down
- Seated forward fold: 75 sec
- Pigeon pose: 45 sec/side
- Quad stretch: 45 sec/side

## Core / Optional Finisher
2 rounds:
- 20 flutter kicks
- 10 supermans

## Coaching Notes
Prioritize knee tracking over toes and full depth squats. Metcon: pace early to avoid burning out legs for later rounds.

### Week-over-Week Progression
- Week 1: Back squat 4x8 @ RPE 7; 16 min AMRAP
- Week 2: Back squat 4x10 @ RPE 7.5 (add 5-10 lbs); 17 min AMRAP
- Week 3: Back squat 5x6 @ RPE 8; KB swing weight up (if good form), same AMRAP
- Week 4: Back squat 3x12 @ RPE 6 (lighter); 12 min AMRAP
